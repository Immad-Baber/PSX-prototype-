Reading a PSX chart
shouldn't need a finance degree.

Pakistani retail investors currently choose between raw, jargon-heavy data terminals (PSX, scstrade, Sarmaaya) or nothing at all — no product currently explains, in plain language, why a stock looks strong, cites its evidence, and tells you when it might be wrong.

View working prototype → See the gap in existing tools
HMM regime detection · GA-optimised indicators · RAG-generated plain-language reports · disconfirmation agent
Hypothesis
No existing PSX platform combines technical + fundamental analysis with plain-language, evidence-cited reasoning and a built-in counter-argument check. If this prototype proves that combination works on one stock end-to-end, scaling it across the KSE-100 is engineering, not research risk.
