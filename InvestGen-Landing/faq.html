Frequently Asked Questions

What makes InvestGen different from competitors?
Unlike standard platforms that rely on fixed technical indicators, InvestGen uses an adaptive multi-agent system. It identifies the current market regime and dynamically adjusts which indicators are most relevant. Furthermore, we provide calibrated confidence scores backed by walk-forward testing, rather than black-box guarantees.
Does InvestGen execute trades automatically?
No. InvestGen is a decision-support and investment intelligence system. Real-money trade execution or brokerage integration is out of scope. We provide the insights, and you execute the trades with your preferred broker.
What is a Disconfirmation Agent?
It is a specialized AI agent within our system designed to find historical situations that looked exactly like the current market but resulted in an opposing outcome. By presenting counter-evidence, we protect investors from overconfidence.
Does InvestGen support markets other than PSX?
Currently, our system is entirely optimized and calibrated for the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX). Multi-market support is not available at this time.
