Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about InvestOPak, explained clearly.

General
What is InvestOPak?
InvestOPak is an AI-powered investment decision support system designed specifically for stocks listed on the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX). Rather than giving you a final answer, it acts as an interactive AI consultant that guides you through the analysis process step by step, asking questions, explaining its reasoning, and letting you make every important decision yourself.
Who is InvestOPak built for?
InvestOPak is designed for retail investors on the PSX, especially those who may not have deep financial knowledge. Instead of assuming you already know which indicatorsMathematical calculations based on price/volume used to predict future trends. to use or what information is relevant, the AI guides you through the process, explains each step in plain language, and only asks you to make the final call.
What makes InvestOPak different from other PSX platforms?
Most existing tools, whether PSXFusion, AlphaGen, or PSXInvest, are one-way signal feeds. They give you an output and you consume it. InvestOPak is different in three key ways: (1) It has a real accept/reject/modify dialogue at every stage so you shape the analysis. (2) It uses HMM-based market regime detection and Genetic Algorithm optimization to select the right indicatorsMathematical calculations based on price/volume used to predict future trends. for the current market condition, not fixed defaults. (3) It includes a Disconfirmation Agent that actively searches for evidence that contradicts the recommendation, and Brier Score / ECE calibrated confidence scores so you know how reliable the output is.
Does InvestOPak support markets other than PSX?
No. InvestOPak is built and calibrated exclusively for the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX). Its HMM regime models, GA-optimized indicatorA mathematical calculation based on price/volume used to predict future trends. parameters, and historical retrieval database are all trained on PSX data. Multi-market support is outside the current project scope.
Does InvestOPak support derivatives, currencies, or commodities?
No. The system is scoped to PSX-listed equities (stocks) only. Derivatives, currencies, forex, and commodities are explicitly outside the project scope.
The AI Screener
What is the AI Screener?
The AI Screener is the main user-facing feature of InvestOPak. It is a conversational interface that acts as your personal investment consultant. It asks about your goals and risk tolerance, recommends relevant analysis options, explains why each option is suggested, and lets you accept, reject, modify, or add to those suggestions at every stage of the analysis.
What questions will the AI Screener ask me?
At the start, the AI will ask about your investment objective (e.g., long-term growth vs short-term trading), your risk tolerance (conservative, balanced, or aggressive), and which PSX stock you want to analyze. Based on your responses, it then guides you through technical, fundamentalThe underlying financial health of a company (revenue, earnings, debt)., and risk analysis, asking targeted follow-up questions at each stage.
Can I change my mind or go back during the analysis?
Yes. The AI Screener is designed to allow you to revisit and change previous choices throughout the session. You are never locked into a path. If you want to add an indicatorA mathematical calculation based on price/volume used to predict future trends. you previously skipped, or explore a risk factor you initially dismissed, you can do so at any point.
Can I add or remove indicatorsMathematical calculations based on price/volume used to predict future trends. from the AI's recommendation?
Yes, that is a core feature. After the AI recommends a set of technical indicatorsMathematical calculations based on price/volume used to predict future trends. based on the detected market regime, you can continue with them, remove any you do not want, add indicatorsMathematical calculations based on price/volume used to predict future trends. the AI did not select, or ask the AI to explain why a particular indicatorA mathematical calculation based on price/volume used to predict future trends. was chosen. The final analysis runs using your chosen set.
Technical Analysis
Which technical indicatorsMathematical calculations based on price/volume used to predict future trends. does InvestOPak support?
InvestOPak supports: SMA, EMA, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, ADX/DMI, OBV, Stochastic Oscillator, ATR, and Candlestick patterns. The system does not apply all of them uniformly. It uses market regime detection to identify which subset is appropriate for current conditions, then lets you customize the final selection.
How does the system decide which indicatorsMathematical calculations based on price/volume used to predict future trends. to recommend?
The system uses a Hidden Markov Model (HMM) to classify the current market as BullishA market state where prices are expected to rise., BearishA market state where prices are expected to fall., or Sideways. Different regimes favour different indicatorsMathematical calculations based on price/volume used to predict future trends. (e.g., RSI and Bollinger Bands are ideal in Sideways markets; MACD and ADX are more useful in strongly trending markets). A Genetic Algorithm (GA) then optimizes the parameters of the selected indicatorsMathematical calculations based on price/volume used to predict future trends. for the specific regime, rather than using the same fixed settings for every market condition.
What is a Market Regime and why does it matter?
A market regime is the overall state of the market: BullishA market state where prices are expected to rise. (prices trending up), BearishA market state where prices are expected to fall. (prices trending down), or Sideways (prices trading in a flat range). It matters because the same indicatorA mathematical calculation based on price/volume used to predict future trends. can give completely different signals in different regimes. RSI, for example, works well in a Sideways market but gives misleading readings in a strong trend. InvestOPak detects the regime first so that only the most relevant indicatorsMathematical calculations based on price/volume used to predict future trends. are recommended.
AI Technology
What is the Disconfirmation Agent?
The Disconfirmation Agent is a mandatory safety layer built into InvestOPak. Instead of only looking for evidence that supports the recommendation, it actively searches historical data for cases where the exact same technical signals led to a loss. If it finds conflicting evidence, it surfaces this to you and asks whether you want to investigate further. This protects you from overconfident AI recommendations.
What is RAG and how does InvestOPak use it?
RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. When the AI encounters a current stock setup, it searches a database of historical PSX market situations to find similar cases. If, for example, the current technical pattern for LUCK closely resembles a setup from 2021, the system retrieves that historical case and explains what happened next. You then decide whether to use that historical evidence in your analysis.
How does the multi-agent architecture work?
InvestOPak uses specialized agents for different tasks: a Data Agent (fetches PSX data), Technical Analysis Agent, FundamentalThe underlying financial health of a company (revenue, earnings, debt). Analysis Agent, Market Context Agent, Risk Agent, Disconfirmation Agent, and CalibrationThe process of adjusting AI parameters to match real-world historical data. Agent. The AI Screener acts as the coordinator, routing your choices to the right agent at each stage and assembling the results into a coherent, interactive analysis flow.
Can I request a What-If scenario analysis?
Yes. The AI may offer, or you can request, a What-If scenario such as "What if inflation rises next month?" or "What if energy prices spike?". The system uses historical data for that specific scenario to adjust the weight of fundamentalThe underlying financial health of a company (revenue, earnings, debt). factors and show you the potential impact on the stock's outlook before it actually happens.

What is the AI Confidence Score?
The AI Confidence Score is a percentage that reflects how strongly aligned the technical signals, fundamentalThe underlying financial health of a company (revenue, earnings, debt). data, and historical patterns are for a given recommendation. It is not a guarantee of profit. It is an indication of certainty under current market conditions. The CalibrationThe process of adjusting AI parameters to match real-world historical data. page shows how reliable these scores have been historically.
How does InvestOPak measure its own accuracy?
InvestOPak runs a calibrationThe process of adjusting AI parameters to match real-world historical data. loop: after each prediction is made, the system waits for the actual market outcome, then compares what it predicted against what happened. It uses two formal metrics: Brier Score (measures accuracy of confidence levels, 0 is perfect) and Expected CalibrationThe process of adjusting AI parameters to match real-world historical data. Error (ECE) (measures the gap between stated confidence and actual accuracy). These are visible on the CalibrationThe process of adjusting AI parameters to match real-world historical data. page.
Is the confidence score a guaranteed return?
Absolutely not. The confidence score is an indication of analytical certainty, not a financial guarantee. Markets are inherently uncertain and past performance does not guarantee future results. InvestOPak provides decision support. You make the final investment decision.
What is Walk-Forward Testing?
Walk-Forward Testing is the method InvestOPak uses to validate its models honestly. Rather than training on all historical data and testing on the same data (which produces inflated results), the model is trained on old data, tested on the immediate next unseen period, then rolled forward. This closely simulates real-world conditions and prevents overfitting.
What InvestOPak Does Not Do
Does InvestOPak execute trades automatically?
No. InvestOPak is purely a decision support system. It does not connect to any broker, execute trades, manage real money, or make any investment decision without your explicit input. The scope explicitly excludes brokerage integration and automated trade execution.
Is InvestOPak a licensed financial advisor?
No. InvestOPak is an academic final year project (BS Software Engineering) and a technology prototype. It is not a registered financial advisor, investment firm, or regulated financial service. All outputs are for informational and educational purposes only. Always consult a qualified financial professional before making investment decisions.
Does InvestOPak have a mobile app?
Not at this time. InvestOPak is a web-based dashboard. A mobile application is outside the current project scope.
What is currently in the prototype versus the full system?
The current prototype is a working frontend demonstration (HTML/CSS/JS) showing the Dashboard, Market Overview, AI Screener conversational flow, Portfolio, All Stocks browser, CalibrationThe process of adjusting AI parameters to match real-world historical data. page, and Watchlist. The prototype does not yet connect to a real backend, real PSX data pipeline, or the multi-agent architecture. All current data is static for demonstration purposes. The full system with HMM, GA, RAG, Disconfirmation Agent, and real-time PSX data is under active development.
