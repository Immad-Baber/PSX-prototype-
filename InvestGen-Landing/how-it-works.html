The Engine Behind the Intelligence

InvestOPak relies on a sophisticated multi-agent reasoning architecture that processes raw market data into high-confidence insights.

1. Data Acquisition Pipeline

We source comprehensive OHLCV and foundational financial-structure data for PSX-listed equities, feeding an automated data engine built for robustness.

2. Regime-Aware Analysis

A Hidden Markov Model (HMM) classifies current market states (e.g., high-volatility, trending bullish). Based on this state, our Genetic Algorithm (GA) adaptively selects and weights the most relevant technical indicators.

3. Disconfirmation & Validation

Before issuing any insight, the Disconfirmation Agent searches historical data for similar indicator patterns that resulted in opposing outcomes, ensuring our confidence scores are realistic.
