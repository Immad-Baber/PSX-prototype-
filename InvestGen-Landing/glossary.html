Glossary of Terms

Definitions of core financial and technical concepts used in the InvestGen platform.

Hidden Markov Model (HMM)
A statistical model used in InvestGen to classify hidden market "regimes" (e.g., bull market, sideways volatility) based on observable data like price and volume.
Genetic Algorithm (GA)
An optimization technique inspired by natural evolution. We use it to dynamically tune technical indicator weights to fit the currently detected market regime.
Expected Calibration Error (ECE)
A metric that measures the difference between an AI model's stated confidence and its actual accuracy over time. Lower ECE means the confidence score is highly reliable.
Walk-Forward Testing
A robust backtesting strategy where the model is continuously retrained on past data and tested on the immediate future data, preventing overfitting.
Disconfirmation Agent
A unique part of the InvestGen pipeline that actively searches for historical evidence that contradicts the primary investment recommendation.
