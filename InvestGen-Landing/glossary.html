Glossary of Terms

Every important word used in InvestOPak, explained in plain language.

Technical IndicatorsMathematical calculations based on price/volume used to predict future trends.
RSI - Relative Strength Index MomentumThe strength or speed of a price trend.
Measures the speed and size of recent price changes on a scale of 0 to 100. When RSI goes above 70, the stock is overbought and may drop. When it falls below 30, it is oversold and may bounce. Think of it like a stretched rubber band: pull it too far and it snaps back.
MACD - Moving Average Convergence Divergence Trend
Compares two moving averages (12-day vs 26-day) to show trend direction and strength. When the short average crosses above the long one it is a BullishA market state where prices are expected to rise. signal. When it crosses below, it is BearishA market state where prices are expected to fall..
Bollinger Bands VolatilityThe rate at which the price of a stock increases or decreases.
Draws three lines on the chart: a middle average, an upper ceiling, and a lower floor. When price hits the upper band it often drops back; when it hits the lower band it often bounces up. Ideal for Sideways (range-bound) markets.
ADX - Average Directional Index Trend Strength
Measures how strong a trend is, regardless of direction. ADX above 25 means the trend is strong and worth following. Below 20 the market is drifting. InvestOPak uses ADX to filter out weak, unreliable signals.
OBV - On-Balance Volume Volume
Uses trading volume to confirm whether a price move is genuine. If OBV rises while price is flat, institutions may be quietly buying before a breakout. Price can be manipulated, but volume rarely lies.
ATR - Average True Range Risk
Measures how much a stock typically moves per day. If LUCK has ATR of Rs 5, it swings around 5 rupees on a normal day. InvestOPak uses ATR to set smart Stop-Loss levels that are wide enough to survive normal daily noise.
SMA - Simple Moving Average Trend
The average closing price over N days. Smooths out daily noise to reveal the underlying trend. A stock trading above its 50-day SMA is generally in an uptrend.
EMA - Exponential Moving Average Trend
Like SMA but gives more weight to recent prices, so it reacts faster to new information. InvestOPak uses EMA inside MACD and RSI calculations for faster, more responsive signals.
AI and Machine Learning
HMM - Hidden Markov Model Core AI
Identifies hidden market regimes (Bull, Bear, Sideways) from observable signals like price and volume. The regime itself is invisible but its effects are not. The HMM reads these patterns and tells the system which indicatorsMathematical calculations based on price/volume used to predict future trends. to prioritize.
Genetic Algorithm (GA) Optimization
Inspired by evolution (survival of the fittest), it tunes indicatorA mathematical calculation based on price/volume used to predict future trends. weights for each market regime, finding the optimal combination of signals that fits current market conditions.
RAG - Retrieval Augmented Generation AI Agent
The AI searches a database of historical market setups to find situations similar to today. If the current setup matches March 2022 at 94% similarity, the AI uses that historical outcome to inform its recommendation.
Disconfirmation Agent Safety Layer
A mandatory AI agent that actively tries to disprove the main recommendation. It searches history for cases where the exact same signals led to a loss. The system's built-in devil's advocate, protecting you from overconfident AI predictions.
What-If Scenario Analysis Simulation
Lets you ask what happens if a specific event occurs - such as inflation rising or oil prices spiking. The AI adjusts the analysis based on historical data for that scenario, showing potential impact before it happens.

Brier Score Metric
Measures how accurate the AI confidence scores are. Ranges from 0 (perfect) to 1 (worst). Our score of 0.142 is strong, meaning our confidence numbers closely match actual real-world outcomes.
ECE - Expected CalibrationThe process of adjusting AI parameters to match real-world historical data. Error Metric
The average gap between stated confidence and actual accuracy. Our 5.8% ECE means our AI confidence claims are only 5.8% off from reality on average. Lower is better.
Walk-Forward Testing Validation
A backtesting method where the model is never allowed to see future data. It trains on old data, predicts the next period, then rolls forward. Prevents overfitting and proves the model can work in real, unseen conditions.
